# Cafe and restaurant design reference

User-approved direction on 2026-11-03: use Korma's design approach as the reference for future cafe and restaurant menus

- Preserve each brand's original logo colors and transparent background; give the centered logo clear prominence
- Use a coherent brand-specific photographic hero, restrained gradients and a complete first-screen composition
- Use short Arabic copy, readable typography and a clear menu action with a visible loyalty entry
- Keep the loyalty summary compact below the hero; explain the stamp count and reward explicitly
- Give offers a dedicated section; keep product categories and cards clean with accurate prices and matching images
- Use purposeful entrance and interaction animation, respect reduced motion and avoid unnecessary loading delays
- Publish verified social accounts and branch location from the brand's source
- For print: individual menu and loyalty A4 posters, original brand identity, large high-contrast QR with quiet zone, vector PDF and 300dpi PNG; decode final image and rendered PDF against the exact live destination
- Apply this direction to new work; do not automatically restyle existing brands or change their products, subscriptions or loyalty terms

Implementation reference: components/menu/signature-menu.tsx, components/menu/signature-menu.module.css and lib/menu/signature-brands.ts
